GPS Tracker - 1S power schematic revision - 2026-10-26
STATUS: DESIGN_REVIEW / NOT FABRICATION READY

Architecture:
J4 external regulated USB 5V -> IP2312 switching charger -> protected 1S pack J3.
J3 -> SW3 hard disconnect -> MT3608 boost 5V -> AMS1117-3.3 -> ESP-12F.
J4 is a 2-pin power input header, NOT an implemented USB-C receptacle or PD interface.
Switch SW3 OFF while charging. No power path/load sharing implemented.
J1 now 3.3V TEST only; do not inject another supply while battery stage is on.
UART J2 remains 3.3V TTL, external programmer, manual FLASH/RESET.

Charger:
Select IP2312 donor verified for standard 4.2V cells, not 4.35V variant.
R8 135k sets about 1A; provisional only. Do not charge until pack specification permits it.
IP2312 has maximum 3A capability; 45k about3A /91k about1.5A; NOT automatic permission to increase charge current.
J5 requires battery-mounted 100k NTC B4100, in parallel with onboard82k per manufacturer example.
Do not leave NTC unplugged. No temperature protection bypass fitted.
J3 requires a protected pack output including protected negative; no onboard bare-cell BMS.
No reverse-polarity circuit implemented. Verify battery connector polarity before assembly.

Boost:
MT3608 reference stage L2=4.7uH / Isat>=3A target, SS34 diode,22uF input/output.
R12/R13=110k/15k => 5.0V nominal with0.6V feedback reference.
Switch disconnects battery from whole load, since boost EN alone does not isolate diode path.
Check donor inductor value/current/DCR, diode package, effective MLCC capacitance and voltage rating.
Validate output at pack low-voltage cutoff, at startup and with ESP WiFi load transients.

LDO:
AMS1117-3.3 SOT-223 chosen for donor availability and larger package than SOT-23.
500mA output capability is NOT thermally qualified: heat=0.85W at500mA,0.34W at200mA.
Input10uF and output22uF tantalum provisional: validate exact donor LDO datasheet ESR/stability requirements.
Total estimated efficiency at90% boost:0.90*(3.3/5)=59.4%, before quiescent losses.
Use adequate copper heatsinking; verify junction and case temperatures.
D1 3.3V PWR LED rotated vertical; existing supply symbol/wiring conventions retained.

PCB/footprints:
Existing PCB not updated/routed. Several new parts intentionally have no footprint until donor dimensions are confirmed (IP2312 EP geometry, inductors, polarized caps, power switch).
IP2312 local symbol pins:1 D1,2 TEST,3 D2,4 NTC,5 BAT,6 ICHG,7 SW,8 VIN,9 exposed GND pad.
Cached AMS1117 symbol flattened from KiCad AP1117 base. Verify actual donor pinout/tab mapping.
Supply traces >=0.20mm minimum is not sufficient evidence of current/thermal adequacy.

Evidence:
Native KiCad CLI 10.0.7 ERC:0 errors0warnings.
Exported native netlist: checked protected pack/charge node, switch/boost feed, diode polarity, feedback divider, LDO output to ESP pin8, isolated charger VBUS vs boosted5V, D1 polarity.
Native SVG reviewed for both sheets; no physical tests, DRC, thermal, RF, battery qualification or production approval.
